Landscape 1536x1024 fine art editorial photograph for his.fm, art direction TIDAL HOURS. A small solitary rocky island with a few wind-shaped pine trees sits slightly right of centre in an immense almost still grey-blue sea. Long low tide ripples, distant horizon almost erased by white maritime fog, a narrow pale soft sky. Island less than one eighth of image area, neither tropical nor fantasy. Muted porcelain grey, slate blue, driftwood green and the palest sand. Delicate real sea and stone detail with extraordinarily generous negative space on left and above. A serene medium-format print, quiet weather, subtle film grain, low visibility and real atmosphere, no dramatic sunrise, no epic tourism composition. The feeling is that the tide can move and a person may keep their own time. No boats, no buildings, no people, no text, no logos, no borders, no watermark. Full bleed, one coherent understated coastal scene.
